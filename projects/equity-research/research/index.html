Research ledger

Every block was registered — hypothesis, statistic and decision rule fixed in a committed document — before it ran. What was committed to comes first; what came out is below it. Nothing here is live evidence.

Every block that ran is listed, including the ones that tested a return no execution could take — that is what a forking-paths ledger is for, and dropping them would be the selective reporting it exists to prevent. But only one leg is reported as a result anywhere else on this site: the implementable one, open t+1 to open t+2. The credited close-to-close return the record books is an accounting convention, not a strategy.
